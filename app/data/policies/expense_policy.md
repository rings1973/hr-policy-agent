# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Work-Related Expenses
The company reimburses reasonable and necessary business expenses that are pre-approved, documented, and aligned to the role's responsibilities. Typical eligible costs include approved travel, conference registration, and required equipment.

## Ineligible Expenses
Personal travel, entertainment, meals not tied to a business purpose, and duplicate purchases are not reimbursable. Expenses that are not pre-approved may be denied unless there is an emergency or required business exception.

## Equipment and Office Setup
Employees may request reimbursement for approved home office equipment if the required purchase aligns with the role and has manager approval. Business justification and an itemized receipt are required.
