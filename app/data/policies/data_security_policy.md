# Data Security and Acceptable Use Policy

## Required Controls
All employees must use approved accounts and multifactor authentication on company systems. Workstations must lock automatically after a period of inactivity, and company devices must avoid unsecured public Wi-Fi for sensitive operations.

## Confidential Data
Confidential employee data, payroll records, and identity information must be accessed only for valid business purposes. Downloading or copying sensitive data to personal devices is prohibited unless explicitly approved by Information Security.

## Remote Work Security
Remote work requires use of approved VPN access, company-managed devices, and secure collaboration tools. Any device not approved for remote work may not be used to access employee benefits or payroll systems.
